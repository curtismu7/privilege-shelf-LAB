# Talk track: Privilege Map LAB

Each `##` heading is one presenter step. Presenter mode shows them in order. Names in the text come from content/cast.json, and customer.config can rename them.

## This is an index

Start with the banner. This page opens links and summarizes content; it makes no calls of its own, holds no tenant and no credential, and nothing leaves this machine. The labs it links to run separately, each with its own simulation banner. If a customer asks "is that your tenant", the answer is no.

## Show the family

Open the Standalone labs page. Forty labs, grouped Demo and Tester, each with one line on what it does. Say the shape of the family: the map and field guide explain the model; the attack labs show what goes wrong without it; the inspector labs drive the gateway; the policy labs show Cedar deciding.

## Open one lab for the room

Pick the lab that fits the audience and click its card: the link opens it through the shared lab proxy in a new tab. The shelf page itself makes no calls; the lab you open does its own thing. Say which lab you would pick for a security room (attack gallery) versus a developer room (agent gateway inspector).

## Show who is accountable

Open the Feature guide and pick Agent Registry. The point is the custodian: the human {{cast.agent}} acts as, who policy is issued to, and who the gateway log names. Say that a key with no accountable human is the problem this solves.

## Be honest about what is unverified

Open "Sources and what is not verified" on any feature before you present it. The content is a draft compiled from documentation and owner tests, and each card lists what was not measured. If it is not on the card as verified, do not claim it.

## Tell a story

Open the Stories page. Pick the story that fits the room: a new agent identity, a call walking the checks, or a refusal that needs diagnosing. The names in the stories come from customer.config, so rename the cast if the audience wants its own vocabulary.

## Reset

Press Reset in the footer between sessions. It clears this workspace's trace and presenter position. Settings stay. Another workspace is untouched.

## Search before you scroll

If the room asks "do we have anything for X", use the search box instead of scrolling. Typing a word filters all forty cards at once: try "audit" or "a2a". Say that every lab also has a catalog id on its card, so you can find the same lab again on the launcher shelf.
